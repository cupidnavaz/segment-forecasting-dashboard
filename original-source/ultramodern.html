⚙ How the Calculations Work

📈 Growth %

((Revenue − Costs) / Costs) × 10

+ (Market Share × 0.3)

− (Churn Rate × 0.5)

+ (Units Sold / 1000)

Higher profit margin + market share + volume = higher growth.

⚔ Risk Level

High if: Profit Margin < 12% OR Churn > 18% OR Competitors > 5

Low if: Margin > 25% AND Churn < 8% AND Competitors < 3

Medium for everything else

Profitability, retention, and competitive pressure determine risk.

😊 Confidence

60 + (Revenue / 50) + (Units / 200) − (Churn × 2)

Capped between 55% and 98%

More revenue and volume = higher confidence. High churn reduces it.
